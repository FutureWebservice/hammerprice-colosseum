## In three sentences

Hammerprice is a marketplace for collectible cards that already exist as tokens on Solana. Buyer and seller both sign one transaction that pays the seller and transfers the card. In auctions we never hold money, cards or keys, and no key of ours can move them. In a chance pack you pay the pack operator directly and first and the operator delivers the card; we never receive that payment, and so we cannot refund it.
